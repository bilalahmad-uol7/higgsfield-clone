- `handle_new_user()` (trigger `on_auth_user_created`; README says it grants 120 signup credits)

- `refund_credits(p_user uuid, p_ref text) returns integer`. Raises `unknown_job`.

- The three credit functions are revoked from public/anon/authenticated and granted only to `service_role`.
